Brand Story

Born in Luxury. Built for the Future.

Every premium experience begins at the table where memorable dinners unfold, important decisions are made, and lasting connections happen. Yet as the world around us grew intelligent, the table remained strangely disconnected, relying on guesswork instead of real insight. That gap inspired WOOBLY. We envisioned a future where every premium table could seamlessly connect people, service, and technology without ever compromising elegance, privacy, or human touch. That vision gave birth to LUXEGENIE, our Intelligent Table Concierge, built to make every premium table truly intelligent.

The Founder

"The most enduring businesses are built by solving meaningful problems."

With over two decades of entrepreneurial experience across India, the UAE, and Africa, Sandeep Modi has built and scaled ventures across retail, manufacturing, international trade, hospitality, and technology.

His entrepreneurial journey has been one of both remarkable growth and valuable lessons—marked by financial setbacks, market disruptions, failed ventures, and the resilience to rebuild from zero more than once. These experiences shaped a powerful conviction that ultimately became the foundation of WOOBLY.

Today, WOOBLY is pioneering the category of Intelligent Table Technology, transforming premium tables into seamless, personalized experiences for luxury hospitality and executive environments.

"The world's most meaningful experiences begin around a table. I believe the table should be as intelligent as the people around it."

— Sandeep Modi, Founder, WOOBLY

01 / Roots & Foundation

Born into an affluent North Indian family, Sandeep Modi always envisioned building a global brand. After completing his post-graduation at Leeds University, UK, he returned to India and scaled his family's retail furnishing business into new heights. Driven by ambition for faster growth, he expanded across the UAE, Africa, and India — building multimillion-dollar businesses and investing in real estate and startups along the way.

02 / Redefining Hospitality

A financial setback during COVID-19 reshaped Sandeep's perspective. Guided by mentors, he came to a defining realization that truly scalable businesses solve real problems through technology. In 2022, he moved to Gurugram to build a new team and founded LUXEGENIE, a Guest Experience Smart DigiPod designed for the top 5% of the luxury dining segment. His vision: to redefine global luxury hospitality through exceptional, personalized guest experiences.

03 / Global Scale

After completing his education at Birla Institute (Pilani), Sydenham College (Mumbai), and Leeds University (UK), Sandeep Modi joined his father's retail furnishing business in 2003 — scaling it significantly over the next six years.

In 2010, he moved to Dubai to explore metal and battery scrap trading, expanding operations across Kenya and South Korea, before returning to India in 2014 with a truly global perspective. That same ambition now drives his vision for WOOBLY — taking LUXEGENIE beyond India, into premium hospitality and executive spaces across the world.

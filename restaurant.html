More than a lamp

The LUXEGENIE smart lamp is engineered to elevate the modern dining experience. By integrating table-side service paging, ambient candlelight intelligence, and custom-tuned lighting scenes, it empowers luxury hospitality venues to offer unmatched guest care and a highly personalized dining atmosphere.

Discreet Interaction

Discreetly connect guests with corporate hospitality, summon servers, request the bill, or select sommelier recommendations without interrupting the meal's rhythm.

Atmospheric Intelligence

Dynamically coordinates warm, glare-free light levels matching course transitions, time of day, and ambient noise to maintain absolute intimacy.

Effortless Coordination

An intuitive glass touch interface built for seamless guest convenience, allowing instant staff calling without visible indicators or disruption.

Premium Metallic Finish

Exquisite metallic finishes that elevate board layouts, matching fine linens, luxury silverware, and high-end restaurant branding.

Integrated Ambient Lighting

Soft candlelight-like illumination tuned to highlight gastronomic presentations while avoiding reflection on crystal glasses and eyes.

Intuitive Top Interface

Water-resistant, easy-to-sanitize tempered glass top interface built for heavy restaurant operations and fluid user adjustments.

Smart Service Connectivity

One-tap silent pagers that directly notify butler stations, floor managers, or wine cellars, preserving guest privacy.

Compact & Elegant Form

An elegant, small footprint base that leaves table layouts open for dishes, pairings, and centerpiece designs.

Customizable Experience

Saves tailored room illumination states for romantic dinners, power lunches, business banquets, or exclusive tastings.

Executive Spaces

Distinctive configurations designed to harmonize with diverse dining environments, guest counts, and table contexts.
